FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before investing with Statlen.

What is the minimum amount I can invest?
+
There is no strict minimum. We welcome investors starting from as little as $500. Our Starter tier begins at $500, Growth at $5,000, and Premium at $50,000. Start with what you are comfortable with and grow from there.
How are my returns paid to me?
+
Returns are paid annually — delivered directly to your bank account, mobile money, or via Paystack. You will receive a full report before every payment showing exactly how your returns were generated.
Is my investment safe and confidential?
+
Yes — 100%. Your investment details, identity, and financial information are never shared with third parties. All transactions are confirmed in writing via email and WhatsApp. We operate under strict confidentiality protocols managed by certified professionals.
Can I withdraw my investment early?
+
Investment terms are discussed during onboarding. Generally investments run for a minimum of 12 months to allow full returns to be generated. Early withdrawal options are available — contact our team to discuss your specific situation.
Where exactly is my money invested?
+
Your capital is invested across Agriculture, Renewable Energy, and Agro-Processing sectors in Nigeria and West Africa. These are real physical assets — farmland, energy infrastructure, and food processing operations — not speculative trading. You receive quarterly reports showing exactly where your money is deployed.
How do I get started?
+
Simply fill in the investment form on our Invest page or send us a WhatsApp message. Our team responds within 24 hours to walk you through the process step by step. There are no complicated forms or long waiting periods.
