+
Returns are paid annually — delivered directly to your bank account, mobile money, or via Paystack. You will receive a full report before every payment showing exactly how your returns were generated.

+
Yes — 100%. Your investment details, identity, and financial information are never shared with third parties. All transactions are confirmed in writing via email and WhatsApp. We operate under strict confidentiality protocols managed by certified professionals.
