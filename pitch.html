THE PROBLEM

Africa is losing billions every year

Two deeply connected problems hold Africa's agricultural wealth back from reaching its true potential.

🌾

For African Producers

Nigeria and West Africa produce some of the world's most valuable commodities — cocoa, sesame, palm oil — yet export 90%+ raw and unprocessed. Foreign processors capture the margin. Africa gets the smallest share of what it grows.

💰

For Investors Seeking Returns

Serious investors across Africa and the diaspora have no reliable, transparent, professionally managed vehicle to participate in Africa's agricultural growth. Banks pay inflation. Markets are volatile. Options are opaque or inaccessible.

The result: Billions in value leaves Africa unprocessed every year — while African investors have no credible vehicle to grow their capital from it.
